## What you are building

A free, public re-analysis that checks how much group work and hands-on teaching help students pass and learn college calculus, using data anyone can download, so anyone can repeat your numbers.

## Why it matters

Calculus is the gate to every science, engineering, and health degree. Many students fail it or drop out of it, and those students often leave STEM for good. Studies say active teaching (students working problems in class, often in small groups, instead of only listening to a lecture) helps. But most of that evidence is locked in papers whose data nobody else can check.

This project takes the studies that did share their raw data, re-runs the analysis in the open, and asks one practical question: how big is the benefit in calculus specifically, and for which students? Think of it as a second inspection of a bridge, done with the blueprints posted on the wall so others can inspect it too.
